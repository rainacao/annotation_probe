# Welcome

In this study you will see a series of charts. For each chart:

1. **A cross appears in the middle of the screen.** Look at the cross. It goes away on its own after a few seconds, and the chart appears in its place.
2. **Answer the question** on the left side of the screen.
3. **Draw if it helps.** You can draw anywhere on or around the chart with your finger, a stylus, or the mouse. Drawing is optional.
4. **Press Next** to go to the next chart.

There is no time limit, so take the time you need.

When you are ready, press **Start**.

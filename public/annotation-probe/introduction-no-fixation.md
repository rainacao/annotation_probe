# Welcome

In this study you will see a series of charts. For each chart:

1. **Answer the question** on the left side of the screen.
2. **Draw if it helps.** You can draw anywhere on or around the chart with your finger, a stylus, or the mouse. Drawing is optional.
3. **Press Next** to go to the next chart.

There is no time limit, so take the time you need.

When you are ready, press **Start**.
